PadClip

A Windows gameplay recorder built with the Lenovo Legion Go and Steam Deck in mind. On Steam Deck, run PadClip in Windows with an Xbox-compatible controller mapping.

↓ Download for Windows

Recording shortcut

LB + RB
Tap both to start. Tap both again to stop and save.

Your first clip

  1. Extract the ZIP, open PadClip.exe, and switch to your game.
  2. Tap LB + RB together, then let go of both bumpers. Recording continues while you play.
  3. Tap LB + RB together again to stop and save. Find your MP4 in Videos \ PadClip.

Choose Change shortcut to bind controller inputs. For a keyboard, choose Settings → Keyboard shortcut. Select keys to press together or choose Next step to add a sequence. Under Settings, you can set Minimize and Close (X) to send PadClip to the system tray independently, and turn sign-in startup on or off. At sign-in, PadClip listens for your shortcut from the tray. To quit from the tray, right-click the PadClip icon and choose Exit.
